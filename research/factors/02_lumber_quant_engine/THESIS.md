## Abstract

This thesis asks whether publicly available, lagged physical and macroeconomic series contain tradable information about subsequent monthly returns on CME Random Length Lumber futures (LBR=F). The contribution is a six-factor pressure model implemented with publication lags, expanding-window IC weights, and free alternative-data injectors.

**Empirical finding: a documented null.** On LBR=F from August 2022, a threshold rule on the composite is negative (~−26% total return, Sharpe ~−0.7). The engine stays NORMAL through the summer 2026 peak and August selloff. A lag-1 storm-damage impulse (IC ≈ +0.41 in one alignment) does not replicate.

Software completeness is not empirical completeness. A confirmatory chapter needs cash lumber and ALFRED vintages.
