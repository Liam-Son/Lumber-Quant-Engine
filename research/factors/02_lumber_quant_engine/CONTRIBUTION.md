# What would actually “shake” this literature

Not a sixth factor. Not a sharper README. A **replicable null with a lag protocol**.

## The claim this project is willing to defend

On CME LBR=F from August 2022 through early September 2026, a six-factor public-data pressure score **does not** produce a positive threshold backtest, **does not** label the 2026 summer high as SHORTAGE, and **does not** label the August selloff as GLUT. A lag-1 storm-damage impulse that once printed IC ≈ +0.41 **does not replicate**.

That sentence is more valuable than a fitted Sharpe on the same window.

## Four rules for commodity alt-data papers

1. Freeze factors and publication lags before touching the return series.
2. Publish the specification that almost worked and then failed.
3. Never count unit tests as economic tests.
4. A silent NORMAL regime during a supply-led spike is a result, not a missing chart.
